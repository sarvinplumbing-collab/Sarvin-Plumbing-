Warning Signs

When should you call a plumber?

Small problems get expensive if you wait. Call us if you notice any of these.

Dripping taps

A constant drip wastes water every day and wears out the fitting.

Low water pressure

Often a blocked line, a failing valve or a hidden leak.

Slow drains

Water that drains slowly is a blockage building up.

Damp walls or ceiling

Patches and peeling paint can mean a concealed pipe is leaking.

Bad smell from drains

A dry trap or broken line lets sewer gas into the house.

Tank overflowing

A faulty float valve wastes water and motor power.

Home Plumbing Tips

Simple habits that prevent big repairs

TIP

Know where your main valve is

In a leak or burst, closing the main valve first saves your floors and walls while help is on the way.

TIP

Keep oil and food out of the sink

Cooking oil hardens inside pipes. Wipe pans and use a sink strainer to stop kitchen blockages.

TIP

Clean the tank regularly

Sediment in overhead and sump tanks reaches your taps and geyser. Regular cleaning keeps water clean.

TIP

Don't ignore a small drip

A small leak today becomes a damp wall or a broken fitting later. Fixing it early costs much less.
